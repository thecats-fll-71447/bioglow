On Saturday, November 14, The Cats competed at **Elm City Robo Fest** in New
Haven — and came home with **two awards** and a spot at the **Connecticut
State Championship** on December 12.

- 🏆 **Robot Design Award** — for the way the team designed, coded, tested,
  and explained Cat-Bot.
- 🏆 **Robot Performance Award** — the highest Robot Game score at the event:
  **340 points**.

The judges especially liked that Cat-Bot **uses its color sensor to identify
each attachment and choose which code to run**.

## What's this blog for?
Between now and State, the team will post here about what they're building,
coding, testing, and learning — in their own words. Sharing our work is a big
part of FIRST LEGO League, and it's how we'll show the judges (and everyone
else) how our ideas changed over time.

Next up: each team member picks a topic and writes a post. Stay tuned! 🐾
